Switch Guide

The switch under each key decides how your keyboard feels and sounds. Every mechanical switch falls into one of three families.

The three switch families

Linear

A smooth, consistent press from top to bottom with no bump. Popular with gamers for fast, repeated presses.

Tactile

A noticeable bump tells your finger the key has registered. A comfortable all-around choice for typing and gaming.

Clicky

A tactile bump plus a sharp click sound. Satisfying for typists, but loud in shared rooms and on voice calls.

Keyboard layouts

Layout size is just as important as the switch. Smaller boards save desk space and give your mouse more room, but you give up keys that you might use every day.
