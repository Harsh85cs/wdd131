Why maintenance matters

Dust, crumbs, and skin oils build up faster than most people think. On a keyboard, debris under the keycaps can make keys feel scratchy, register twice, or stop working. Inside a PC, dust clogs fans and heatsinks, which raises temperatures and makes the fans spin louder. A few minutes of regular care keeps your gear quieter, cooler, and working longer.
